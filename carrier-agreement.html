AWL Carrier Service Agreement

Alphaway Logistics LLC (“AWL”) provides dispatch and logistical support services to carriers operating under their own authority and/or operating as a legal carrier entity. This Agreement governs the relationship between AWL and the Carrier for dispatch services, onboarding, communication, and freight coordination.

1. Dispatch fee

During the introductory period, AWL’s dispatch fee is 8% of gross on each load AWL dispatches. If the relationship continues beyond the introductory period, AWL’s dispatch fee is 10% of gross on each load AWL dispatches, unless a different written rate is agreed by AWL in writing.

AWL does not act as a factoring company, quick-pay provider, or payment intermediary. Payment timing for broker remittance, factoring, or other third-party payment processes is separate from AWL’s dispatch service and is not guaranteed by AWL.

2. Payment terms

Carrier compensation and freight payment terms are determined by the broker, shipper, or the applicable carrier payment arrangement. AWL is not responsible for guaranteeing broker/shipper payment, quick pay, or invoice processing beyond the dispatch services specifically engaged by AWL. AWL will communicate approved settlement information when provided by the broker or payment source, but AWL does not assume responsibility for any delay, deduction, nonpayment, or dispute from a broker, shipper, factor, or other third party.

3. Carrier responsibilities

The Carrier is responsible for maintaining:

  • valid operating authority, legal entity status, and all required registrations
  • insurance coverage as required by law and by the broker/shipper contract
  • accurate equipment, driver, and availability information
  • compliance with DOT, FMCSA, and all applicable state and federal requirements
  • timely communication regarding delays, equipment issues, safety concerns, route changes, and detention

4. AWL responsibilities

AWL will provide dispatch coordination, load matching support, communication management, and operational coordination for loads accepted under this Agreement. AWL does not control third-party broker payment, trailer availability, shipper scheduling, or carrier operations beyond the dispatch services specified in writing.

5. Authorization and agency

By signing this Agreement, the Carrier authorizes AWL to act as the Carrier’s dispatch and communication representative for loads accepted under this Agreement. AWL may communicate with brokers, shippers, drivers, and other parties on the Carrier’s behalf regarding dispatching, load coordination, schedule updates, and required documentation necessary to move a load. Carrier remains ultimately responsible for compliance, legal operation, and execution of each load.

6. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement upon written notice. Upon termination, AWL’s right to manage and receive dispatch commissions on outstanding loads or assigned agreements remains limited to the terms of the specific load assignment and any written fee arrangement in effect at the time of termination. AWL may discontinue dispatch support for future loads immediately upon written notice if the Carrier fails to comply with this Agreement, is nonresponsive, is not legally authorized to operate, or fails to maintain required documents or insurance.

7. Required documents

Carrier must maintain all required onboarding documents on file, including but not limited to: legal company information, MC/DOT authority details, W-9, COI insurance, factoring/NOA if applicable, equipment list, preferred lanes/rates, dispatch preferences, and signed onboarding forms. Failure to provide these documents may prevent AWL from booking, dispatching, or communicating on the Carrier’s behalf.

8. Confidentiality and records

AWL will keep company records and carrier submissions in AWL-controlled email, storage, and workflow systems. AWL will not publish carrier records, signed agreements, W-9 data, COIs, or private documents in public repositories or unsecured public websites. All private company records should be stored in company-controlled systems and protected according to AWL policy.

9. Limitation of liability

AWL is not responsible for losses, delays, damages, shortages, claims, detention, or issues arising from broker performance, shipper delays, weather, equipment breakdowns, road conditions, labor issues, or payment disputes from third parties. AWL’s liability is limited to the actual fees paid or due under this Agreement for the specific load or service giving rise to the claim, if applicable.

10. Acceptance

By submitting this Agreement through the AWL carrier onboarding process, the Carrier acknowledges that they read, understand, and agree to the terms set forth herein. AWL may maintain the signed agreement and supporting records in AWL-controlled systems for operational and compliance purposes.
